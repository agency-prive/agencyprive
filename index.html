WHAT GUIDES US

Our mission and vision.

01

Our mission

To bring transparency, trust, and structure to the global agency industry.

We want agency discovery to move from fragmented social searches and word-of-mouth into a structured experience where people can understand who an agency is, what it offers, how it is reviewed, whether it is verified, and how to contact it.

02

Our vision

To become the world's leading destination for discovering and evaluating agencies.

The long-term product evolves from an OFM/creator directory into a global agency marketplace spanning creator management, talent, influencer, marketing, social, PR, production, and other specialized agencies.
